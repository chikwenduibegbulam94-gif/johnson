MY STORY

A journey of learning and building.

Ideas become meaningful when we put them to work.

My journey is shaped by consistency, curiosity, entrepreneurship, and the willingness to try something new. Through Brainbuilder Computers and Tech Hub, I have been involved in ICT services, printing, digital support, and helping people develop practical technology skills.

My web development journey is another step in that growth. After seeing a login-page project shared by CodeWithGaurav on Facebook, I decided to practise by creating my own version using HTML and CSS. That experience reminded me that progress begins when we move from watching to doing.

I continue to learn, practise, and build projects that help me improve. My long-term vision is to grow at the intersection of technology, entrepreneurship, and digital empowerment.
